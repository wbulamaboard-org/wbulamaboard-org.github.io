ABOUT US

West Bengal Ulama Board (WBUB) is a religious, educational and social organisation under the Wayejin E Furfura Sharif. We work for unity of Ulama, Islamic education, social reform and welfare of the community.

MEMBER RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Work for the welfare of Islam and society.
  • Maintain unity and respect among Ulama.
  • Support educational, social and humanitarian projects.
  • Follow the rules and guidelines of the organisation.
